    📖 Glossary

    Call Options:

    Contracts giving you the right to BUY stock at a set price. You profit when stock price goes UP. Higher call volume = bullish sentiment.

    Put Options:

    Contracts giving you the right to SELL stock at a set price. You profit when stock price goes DOWN. Higher put volume = bearish sentiment.

    C/P Ratio (battle bar):

    Calls ÷ Puts by volume. Above 1.0 = more call volume (bullish tilt). This is what the battle bar shows.

    Put/Call Ratio (PCR):

    Classic PCR = Puts ÷ Calls (inverse of C/P). PCR >1.0 = more puts. Extremes often fade — but our UI labels C/P, not PCR.

    Premium Flow:

    Total money spent on options (price × volume × 100). Shows real conviction - where smart money is betting.

    0DTE (Zero Days To Expiration):

    Options expiring TODAY. Extreme time decay, high risk/reward. Moves fast - perfect for day trading.

    Net Flow:

    Call volume minus Put volume. Positive = bullish bias, Negative = bearish bias. Large flows = strong directional signal.

    Open Interest (OI):

    Total open contracts. High OI at a strike = support/resistance level. Shows where traders expect price to go.

    🎯 Trading Strategies

    1. Follow the Flow:

    When premium flow is heavily one-sided (>$10M), smart money is betting big. Consider following their direction.

    2. Fade Extreme Ratios:

    When classic PCR >2.0 (too bearish) or <0.5 (too bullish) — i.e. battle C/P <0.5 or >2.0 — markets often reverse. Look for contrarian opportunities.

    3. Watch the Battle Bar:

    The visual battle shows real-time sentiment. When one side "pushes" hard, momentum is strong. Trade with momentum.

    4. Time Your Entries:

    0DTE options decay fastest in last 2 hours. Enter early, exit before 3 PM ET to avoid time decay.

    5. Use ML Signals:

    Our AI analyzes 8+ factors. When confidence >75%, it's a strong signal. Combine with your own analysis.
